The Service is an interval timer for parents using a graduated check-in sleep-training approach. It is a timer only. It is not a baby monitor, a medical device, a health monitor, a safety device, or a substitute for parental supervision.

The Service is intended for use by adults and parents only. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and it is not intended for use by children. A parent or guardian is responsible for all use of the Service on their device, and for all decisions about their baby’s sleep, care, and safety.

3. General information only – no medical advice

The Service provides general information only. It is not medical advice, health advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a recommendation for any particular baby, and it does not create a doctor-patient, clinician-patient, or other professional relationship between you and us.

Talk to your child’s pediatrician or other qualified healthcare professional before starting any sleep-training method, before changing your baby’s sleep routine, and before relying on any interval schedule in the Service. Ask whether a method is appropriate for your baby’s age, health, development, and circumstances, including prematurity, reflux, breathing or feeding difficulties, or any other medical condition. Follow your healthcare professional’s guidance if it differs from anything in the Service, and follow current safe-sleep guidance from your healthcare professional and public health authorities.

No sleep outcome is promised or guaranteed. Results vary by baby, and the Service does not promise that a baby will self-soothe, sleep through the night, sleep longer, or improve on any particular timeline.

4. Do not rely on the Service alone – safety warning

Do not rely on the Service alone to monitor, supervise, or protect your baby. Timers, alarms, and notifications may not work as expected if the device is off, silenced, muted, in low-power or do-not-disturb mode, out of battery, or if notifications are blocked, permissions are denied, or the app is closed. 7. Advertising – Google AdMob, in detail

The Service displays banner advertisements served by Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. The ad SDK is initialized when the app launches, and a banner ad is shown in the app, separate from the timer.

For advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, Google AdMob may collect, use, and share:

  • Your device’s IP address, which may be used to estimate general location
  • Device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID, and, if applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device
  • App product interactions, such as app launch, taps, ad views, and video views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage

Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). AdMob may share limited end-user data with ad networks and bidders to help decide whether to serve an ad, which ad to show, and how much to bid, including the app name and identifier, app category and language, device type and screen size, approximate geographic location, and a truncated IP address.

Your timer logs and preferences are not sent to AdMob as part of an ad request. AdMob’s collection is limited to the advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention data described above, as governed by Google’s policies.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s AdMob data information. If you do not want personalized advertising, you can manage advertising settings on your Android device, including resetting or deleting your advertising ID and opting out of ad personalization, and you can use Google’s ad settings controls. Those controls are provided by Google, not by us, and we cannot guarantee their effect on any particular ad.
